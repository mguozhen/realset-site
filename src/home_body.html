How it works

One pipeline for environments, trajectories and expert judgment, with a verifier at every step.

01

Define task and reward

Task family, environment, what counts as done: tests, business outcomes or an expert rubric.

02

Capture

Agents run in the environment through our gateway; experts and skilled workers produce demonstrations and judgments. Our AI recruiter screens them for the exact task.

03

Verify

Tests run, rubrics are scored, an independent reviewer checks every record. Failures are named and counted, not averaged away.

04

Deliver and iterate

JSONL, LeRobot or your schema, de-identified, hashed, with a live pipeline dashboard. The next batch targets what your model got wrong.

Quality control you can audit

Every record ships with the evidence that it was checked. Buyers get the numbers, not a promise.

01 · Verifier first

A record is only done when the check passes

Coding tasks carry fail-to-pass and pass-to-pass tests. Business tasks carry the real outcome. Judgment tasks carry a written rubric scored item by item. Nothing enters a batch on a reviewer's feeling.

02 · Independent review

A second person, with the recording

The reviewer is never the producer. In the Workspace they see the answer, the evidence and the screen recording of the work, and record a decision that stays attached to the record.

03 · Structural checks

Every trajectory is validated by machine

Tool calls paired with results, schema conformance, duplicate-turn ratio, truncated or interrupted runs flagged, tool names normalized against the original. Sessions that fail are excluded and counted.

04 · Integrity

SHA-256 on every file and the bundle

A manifest lists every object with its size and hash. You verify the delivery yourself; a 793-object embodied pack is checked file by file before it leaves our storage.

05 · De-identification with a receipt

Scrubbed at source, counted per record

Emails, secrets, IPs, paths, usernames, organisation and person names are replaced with consistent placeholders before data leaves our storage. Each record reports how many replacements were made, so a reviewer can see what was touched.

06 · Per-batch QC report

Pass rate, resample rate, exclusions

Each delivery includes metadata.json: per-session model, turns, tool calls, usage coverage, what was captured and what was not. Failures are named and counted, never averaged away.
