Three ways we produce ground truth

Every dataset starts with a real person doing a real task in a real place. We structure what they did into training data models can learn from.

Body

Embodied data for physical policy models

Egocentric and third-person capture of skilled workers performing manipulation tasks in homes, kitchens, warehouses and light assembly lines. Bimanual teleoperation episodes with synchronized stereo video, IMU and action logs. Dense action-level annotation optimized for VLA training.

  • Egocentric video, per hour, task-labeled
  • Teleop episodes, QC'd, per episode
  • Temporal segmentation and language labels
Explore Body → Field

RL environments built from real workflows

Environments that mirror how work actually happens: e-commerce operations, customer support, logistics dispatch, manufacturing SOPs. Domain experts generate trajectories, preference data and rubrics inside the environment, so agentic models learn the task, not the benchmark.

  • Expert trajectories and preference pairs
  • Verifiable rewards from real business outcomes
  • Bilingual English / Chinese expert pools
Explore Field → Judge

Expert evaluation for agents in production

Evaluation design, failure diagnosis and continuous monitoring by people who do the job the agent is replacing. We find where the agent fails, produce targeted training data for the top failure modes, and re-score as models and prompts change.

  • Rubrics grounded in real workflows
  • Human review beyond LLM-as-judge
  • Targeted fix-data for top failure modes

How it works

A capture network of real workplaces and a controlled studio, run as one pipeline with quality measured at every step.

01

Scope the task

We define the task family, environment, embodiment and success criteria with your research team.

02

Recruit demonstrators

Our AI recruiter screens skilled workers for the exact task. Experts, not generalist annotators.

03

Capture and structure

Head-mounted and third-person rigs, or teleop in our studio. Segmentation, labels and QC follow the same day.

04

Deliver and iterate

De-identified data lands in your US cloud bucket with a live pipeline dashboard. Next batch adapts to what your model got wrong.

The Realset Workspace

Where domain experts answer real tasks, record how they got there, pass independent review, and get paid. Every approved record exports as JSONL with provenance you can verify.

Capture

Answer, evidence, recording

Experts contribute an answer, supporting evidence and, when the task calls for it, a screen recording of the work, stored with a SHA-256 digest.

Review

Independent quality gate

A separate reviewer scores correctness, evidence and completeness against the rubric. Only approved records leave the workspace.

Global

English, 简体中文, 日本語, 한국어, Español, العربية. Payouts in USDT, USDC, BTC, IBAN, PayPal, Wise or Alipay, tracked on a ledger.
